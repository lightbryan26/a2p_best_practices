1. Overview

A2P 10DLC (Application-to-Person 10-Digit Long Code) registration is a mandatory carrier requirement for businesses sending SMS messages to U.S. mobile numbers. Registration is enforced by U.S. cellular carriers (Verizon, AT&T, T-Mobile) to verify sender legitimacy, consent integrity, and message transparency.

Key Components

  • Brand Registration: Identifies who is sending messages
  • Campaign Registration: Defines what type of messages are being sent
  • Consent Documentation: Proves how users opt-in to receive messages

2. Campaign Details

Recommended Use Case

Low Volume Mixed - Best for most small businesses sending appointment confirmations, reminders, and occasional promotions.

Use Case Description Template

This campaign sends appointment information - confirmation & reminder messages to customers once they have booked an appointment with [Company Name LLC] (DBA - [Company Name]) on the website (https://website.com) and opted in to receive inquiry, estimates, service updates, and notification SMS from [Company Name].

Use Case Description Requirements

  • Specify the company's legal name (LLC, Inc., etc.)
  • Include DBA if applicable
  • Include the exact website URL
  • List specific SMS types (inquiry, estimates, service updates, notifications)
  • Mention opt-in requirement

3. Consent & Opt-In Requirements

Critical Rules

Rule Requirement
Separate Checkboxes Marketing messages and Non-marketing messages must have separate checkboxes
Unchecked by Default All consent checkboxes must be unchecked by default
Optional Consent Users cannot be forced to opt-in to SMS to submit a form
Separate from Privacy/TnC SMS opt-in checkbox must be separate from Privacy Policy/Terms checkbox

Two Consent Checkbox System

Checkbox #1 - Marketing Messages:

I agree to receive recurring automated promotional and personalized marketing text messages from [Company Name] at [Phone Number]. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message & data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

Checkbox #2 - Non-Marketing Messages:

I agree to receive non-marketing text messages from [Company Name] about my inquiry/estimate/appointments. Message & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

Consent Rules Summary

DO:
  • ✅ Checkboxes must be unchecked by default
  • ✅ Consent must be optional (not required to submit form)
  • ✅ Must have separate checkboxes for marketing vs. non-marketing
  • ✅ SMS consent must be separate from Privacy Policy/Terms consent
DON'T:
  • ❌ Cannot force SMS opt-in even when phone number is required
  • ❌ Cannot pre-select or pre-check consent boxes

4. Website & Form Requirements

Mandatory Elements

Element Location Requirement
Privacy Policy Link Footer Must be visible and accessible
Terms & Conditions Link Footer Must be visible and accessible
Consent Checkboxes Form Unchecked by default, optional
Phone Number Field Form Can be required, but SMS consent must remain optional

Privacy Policy & Terms Requirements

  • Must be clearly visible in footer
  • Must not be obscured by other design elements or pop-ups
  • Must be accessible without extra steps (no login required)
  • Contact information must match Brand Registration
Privacy Policy & Terms Must NOT Mention:
  • ❌ Affiliate marketing
  • ❌ Selling or buying of leads
  • ❌ Third-party data sharing for marketing

Form Configuration

When Phone Number is REQUIRED:

  • Consent checkboxes must be optional
  • Consent checkboxes must be unchecked by default
  • User must be able to submit form without checking SMS consent

When Phone Number is OPTIONAL:

  • Consent checkboxes are not required
  • Privacy Policy and Terms still required in footer

5. Message Flow / Call to Action

The Message Flow describes how users opt-in to receive SMS messages. This is one of the most critical sections for approval.

Required Elements

  • ✅ Specific URL where opt-in occurs
  • ✅ Form name (e.g., "Get a free quote" form)
  • ✅ Description of consent checkboxes (marketing vs. non-marketing)
  • ✅ Statement that checkboxes are unchecked by default
  • ✅ Brand/company identification
  • ✅ "Message & data rates may apply"
  • ✅ Opt-out instructions (STOP)
  • ✅ Help instructions (HELP)

Message Flow Template

End users opt in on https://[website.com] by submitting the "[Form Name]" form. Users enter their phone number and must manually check one of the consent checkboxes: (1) Marketing texts and/or (2) Non-marketing texts (appointment/estimate updates). Checkboxes are unchecked by default. After checking a box and submitting the form, users may receive SMS from [Company Name]. Reply STOP to opt out; HELP for help; Message & data rates may apply.

Alternative: Simple Message Flow

Customers provide opt-in specifically when submitting a form on our website at https://[website.com]. Opt-in is a self-service process where users complete a form that collects their phone number and select a consent checkbox to agree to receive SMS messages.

Website Opt-In Language Template

"By submitting, you authorize [Company Name] to send text messages with [appointment confirmations, reminders, and promotional offers]. Message/data rates apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Reply STOP to unsubscribe."

6. Sample Messages

Requirements

  • ✅ Greeting with recipient's name
  • ✅ Sender identification (person name + company name)
  • ✅ Phone number in +1 format (e.g., +18031234567)
  • ✅ "Reply STOP to unsubscribe"
  • ✅ Use full URLs (https://)

Restrictions

  • ❌ No public URL shorteners (bit.ly, TinyURL, etc.)
  • ❌ No unsecured URLs (must use https://)
  • ❌ No PII (Personal Identifiable Information) beyond examples

Sample Message #1 Template (Appointment Confirmation)

Hi [Name]! This is [Owner/Rep] from [Company Name]. Our appointment for [Date] [Time] is confirmed. Please reach out to +1[PhoneNumber] in case you need to reschedule. Reply STOP to unsubscribe.

Example:

Hi John! This is Mike from SealPro Insulation Solutions. Our appointment for July 20 11:00 AM is confirmed. Please reach out to +15551234567 in case you need to reschedule. Reply STOP to unsubscribe.

Sample Message #2 Template (Follow-Up/Reschedule)

Hi [Name]! This is [Owner/Rep] from [Company Name]. I see you weren't able to confirm our scheduled [appointment type]. Need to reschedule? Book your next appointment at https://[website.com]. Reply STOP to unsubscribe.

Example:

Hi Sarah! This is Mike from SealPro Insulation Solutions. I see you weren't able to confirm our scheduled estimate. Need to reschedule? Book your next appointment at https://get.sealpro.com. Reply STOP to unsubscribe.

Sample Message #3 Template (Reminder)

Good morning! This is [Company Name]. Your [appointment type] is scheduled for today at [Time]. Please [any instructions]. Questions? Call +1[PhoneNumber]. Reply STOP to unsubscribe.

Sample Message #4 Template (Promotion)

Hi [Name]! [Seasonal greeting] [Company Name] is offering [promotion details]. Schedule yours at https://[website.com]. Reply STOP to unsubscribe.

7. Opt-In Message

The Opt-In Message is sent to users immediately after they consent to receive SMS.

Opt-In Message Template

You have successfully opted-in to receive notification and promotional SMS from [Company Name]. Please reply STOP if you need to Opt-out in the future.

Alternative (More Detailed)

You have successfully opted in to receive SMS messages from [Company Name] regarding your inquiry, estimates, service updates, and notification SMS. Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe.

9. Common Rejection Reasons & Fixes

Twilio Error Codes

Error Code Reason How to Fix
30909 CTA/Message Flow incomplete or unverifiable Describe ALL opt-in methods with full detail; include required disclosures; provide accessible proof
30891 Invalid/unverifiable website Ensure website is live, accessible, and has visible opt-in flow
30892 Public URL shortener in sample message Use full URLs or branded short domains only (no bit.ly, TinyURL)
30896 Opt-in error Detail all opt-in methods; include Privacy Policy & Terms links; avoid third-party sharing language
30894 Invalid brand information Ensure brand info matches campaign and website information
30882 Terms & Conditions issue Remove any affiliate marketing or lead buying/selling language
30885 High Risk Campaign flagged as fraud or deceptive - contact support
30897 Disallowed Content Prohibited content detected - contact support

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  1. Pre-checked consent boxes - Always unchecked by default
  2. Single checkbox for all consent - Must separate marketing vs. non-marketing
  3. Missing Privacy Policy/Terms links - Must be visible in footer
  4. Using URL shorteners - Use full URLs only
  5. Phone number format - Use +1 format
  6. Missing STOP instructions - Every message needs opt-out language
  7. Forced SMS consent - Consent must be optional even if phone is required
  8. Affiliate/lead selling language - Remove from Privacy Policy & Terms
  9. Inaccessible opt-in flow - Must be publicly viewable (no login required)
  10. Mismatched business information - Brand, campaign, and website info must match

11. Pre-Launch Website Guidelines

If the website is not yet live, follow these steps:

  1. State in campaign description that it's a pre-launch website
  2. Provide a publicly accessible URL to a screenshot of the SMS opt-in flow
  3. Screenshot must show:
    • The form with phone number field
    • Both consent checkboxes (unchecked)
    • Checkbox language visible
    • Privacy Policy & Terms links in footer

Pre-Launch Message Flow Example

End users opt in via our pre-launch website form by visiting https://[website.com], as we are currently rebuilding the old website as part of optimizing our company. By completing a form that collects their phone number, users can optionally select a consent checkbox to agree to receive SMS messages. Checking this checkbox confirms their consent to receive updates about their [service type] and exclusive offers, ensuring compliance with privacy regulations and transparent communication.

12. Checklist Before Submission

Brand Registration

  • Legal company name matches IRS CP 575 / EIN
  • EIN is correct (use CP 575 or 147c letter)
  • Business address is accurate
  • Business type is correct (LLC, Corp, etc.)

Website Requirements

  • Website is live and accessible
  • Privacy Policy link visible in footer
  • Terms & Conditions link visible in footer
  • Privacy Policy does NOT mention lead selling/buying or affiliate marketing
  • Contact info in Privacy Policy matches Brand Registration
  • Opt-in form is publicly accessible (no login required)

Form Requirements

  • Two separate consent checkboxes (marketing & non-marketing)
  • Checkboxes are unchecked by default
  • SMS consent is optional (not required to submit)
  • Checkbox language includes all required disclosures
  • Phone number field present

Campaign Details

  • Use Case Description follows template
  • Company name includes legal entity (LLC, Inc.)
  • DBA included if applicable
  • Website URL is correct and uses https://

Sample Messages

  • Include greeting with name
  • Include sender name and company name
  • Phone number in +1 format
  • "Reply STOP to unsubscribe" included
  • No URL shorteners
  • All URLs use https://

Message Flow

  • Specific opt-in URL included
  • Form name specified
  • Both checkbox types mentioned
  • "Unchecked by default" stated
  • STOP, HELP, and rates disclaimer included

Opt-In Message

  • Company name included
  • STOP instructions included
